Gas disposition
  Files: data/raw/rrc/olf102 (oil) and data/raw/rrc/gsf102 (gas)
  Record counts file: data/raw/rrc/record.counts (49068 oil rows, 101831 gas rows)
  Page: https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/data-sets-available-for-download/
  Same field positions as src/load_permian.py. One vented-or-flared column. Latest month on the tape for each lease.
  Tape district 14 is the district these counties join to. Its gas rows include the Lipscomb and Panhandle West fields, and the wells that match those lease numbers sit in these counties.
  The Permian loader reads the same tapes and maps tape 09 to RRC 7C, tape 10 to RRC 08, and tape 11 to RRC 8A. This build uses tape district 14 and labels it RRC District 10.
  Checked against the Permian tape row oil district 10 lease 51695, flared_mcf 5324600.

Well locations
  Surface LAT83/LONG83 from the RRC well shapefiles, files wellNNN.zip, layer *s.dbf.
  https://mft.rrc.texas.gov/link/d551fb20-442e-4b67-84fa-ac3f23ecabb4
  Downloaded 2026-09-27 into data/raw/rrc/wells/.

API / lease join
  Statewide API extract folder 2026-09-23, files maf016.ccNNN stored as data/raw/rrc/api/ccNNN.
  https://mft.rrc.texas.gov/link/701db9a3-32b5-488d-812b-cd6ff7d0fe85
  A lease is drawn only when the lease number and the lease name agree, same test as the Permian loader, limited to tape district 14.
  Every district 14 gas row on this tape, for months 2024-08 through 2026-07, has 0 in the vented-or-flared column. Oil rows have non-zero values. The map uses the latest month, which leaves 8 oil leases.
  The point is the mean of surface wells on that lease whose coordinates fall inside these county polygons.

Pipelines and transmission
  Clipped from db/us_pipelines.sqlite tables pipelines and transmission_lines.
  A line is included only when a vertex falls inside one of these Texas county polygons, so Oklahoma, Kansas, and New Mexico segments that miss the polygons are left out.
  Tie-ins: data/panhandle/tieins.geojson is an empty collection. src/load_permian.py also writes no tie-in points from these sources.
